# pythonporfolio
AP Comp Sci Projects

albums.py: An application desgined to help users discover their next favorite album based on their current musical tastes, mood, or preferred genres.

dog_breeder.py & images.py: A duo of projects dedicated to helping prospective pet owners find the perfect canine companion.

hogwarts.py: An interactive questionnaire that channels the magic of Harry Potter Sorting Hat to assign characters into Gryffindor, Hufflepuff, Ravenclaw, or Slytherin.

nickname.py: Helps user discover which iconic hero from the Avengers or the Justice League best aligns with their personality, values, and preffered superpowers.

pokemon.py: A lightweight, turn-based battle game inspired by the classic Pokemon franchise.
